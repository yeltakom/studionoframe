A studio that takes the shape of the work it houses.

Studio No Frame is a Berlin-based studio working across art direction, exhibition design, art production, project management, curation and consulting. We work with artists, cultural institutions, NGOs, project spaces and universities on interdisciplinary and socially engaged projects.

Our structure is flexible and our team is mixed by design. Each project starts from its own material: the works, the building, the people who will move through it. We value collective thinking, experimental methods and process-oriented making, and we plan production so that material and budget are spent where visitors actually feel them.

Partners

Haus der Kulturen der Welt, Istanbul Foundation for Culture and Arts (İKSV), Istanbul Modern, Museum Ludwig, MAXXI Rome, Pera Museum, SALT, La Biennale di Venezia, the Istanbul Biennial, the Istanbul Design Biennial and the Timișoara Biennial. Artists we have worked with include Abbas Zahedi, Ahmet Öğüt, Amy Lien & Enzo Camacho, Ayşe Erkmen, Füsun Onur, Gülsün Karamustafa, Leman Darıcıoğlu, Monira Al Qadiri, Raul Walch and Tomás Saraceno.

Team

The studio is led by Yelta Köm, an Istanbul-born architect and artist who has lived and worked in Berlin for over a decade. His practice centres on spatial interventions and temporary setups built with institutions, artists and communities.

We work with a rotating group of designers, researchers and production managers, and we bring in collaborators to suit each exhibition rather than fitting every show to a fixed team.
