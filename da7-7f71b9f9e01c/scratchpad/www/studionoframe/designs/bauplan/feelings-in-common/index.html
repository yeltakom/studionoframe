Feelings in Common

Ref A03
Venue Pera Museum, Istanbul
Year 2025
Role Exhibition Design & Installation Management
Views 10

Exhibition architecture for Feelings in Common: Works from the British Council Collection, curated by Ulya Soley, organized around the show’s three chapters — “Preserving Care,” “Familiar Faces,” and “Future Perfect.” The spatial design shaped the galleries as chambers of resonance, where works by 29 artists — among them Lucian Freud, David Hockney, Sarah Lucas, Wolfgang Tillmans, Sonia Boyce, and Gilbert & George — could meet across generations and mediums.
