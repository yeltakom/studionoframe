In Search of Vera Molnár

Venue
Pera Museum, Istanbul
Year
2024
Role
Exhibition Design & Installation Management

Exhibition design and installation for In Search of Vera Molnár at Pera Museum, a tribute to the pioneering artist of computer art. The spatial arrangement harmonizes Molnár’s historical works with pieces by contemporary artists inspired by her algorithmic approach.
