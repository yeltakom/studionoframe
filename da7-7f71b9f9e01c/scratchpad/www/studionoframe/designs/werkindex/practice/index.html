We build the rooms exhibitions happen in.

Studio No Frame works across exhibition architecture, art production, project management and curation, with artists, museums, biennials, project spaces and universities. Each project starts from its own material: the works, the building, and the people who will move through it.

We plan production so that material and budget are spent where visitors actually feel them, and we bring in collaborators to suit each exhibition rather than fitting every show to a fixed team.

Register

Nothing we build outlasts the run of the show, so every exhibition is entered in the register with a permanent number, counted from the first one in 2014. The number never changes and never gets reused — a show can be cited years later by its number alone.

Led by

Yelta Köm, an Istanbul-born architect and artist who has lived and worked in Berlin for over a decade.

Worked with

Haus der Kulturen der Welt, Pera Museum, SALT, Istanbul Modern, Museum Ludwig, MAXXI, La Biennale di Venezia, the Istanbul Biennial and İKSV. Artists include Ahmet Öğüt, Ayşe Erkmen, Füsun Onur, Gülsün Karamustafa, Marcel Dzama, Monira Al Qadiri, Åsa Jungnelius and Tomás Saraceno.
