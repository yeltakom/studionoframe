Partial Weaves: Unfinished Stories of Displacement

Venue
TOP e.V., Berlin
City
Berlin
Year
2022
Kind
Project space
Role
Exhibition Designer & Researcher

An exhibition at TOP Project Space exploring undocumented migration along the Pakistan–Iran and Iran–Turkey borders, focusing on the socio-political narratives that emerge around displacement, labor, and border dynamics. Drawing from four years of research under the Topological Atlas project, the spatial concept emphasized the layered, archival nature of displacement stories.
