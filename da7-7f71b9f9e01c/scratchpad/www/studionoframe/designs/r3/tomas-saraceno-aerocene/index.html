Aerocene with Tomás Saraceno

Venue
Studio Tomás Saraceno — Art Basel Miami, Palais de Tokyo, Tanya Bonakdar Gallery
Year
2018–2019
Role
Project Leader, Architect, Designer & Visual Researcher
Thread
Commons

Projects with Studio Tomás Saraceno and the Aerocene Foundation, including Aerocene Albedo at Art Basel Miami (2018), ON AIR — Carte Blanche à Tomás Saraceno at Palais de Tokyo, Paris (2018), Solar Rhythms at Tanya Bonakdar Gallery, New York (2018), and On the Disappearance of Clouds (2019). Aerocene is a global community building a new ecology of practice towards an ethical collaboration with the atmosphere — free from borders, free from fossil fuels.
