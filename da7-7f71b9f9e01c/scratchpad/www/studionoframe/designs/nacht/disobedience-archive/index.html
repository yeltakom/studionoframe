05SALT Beyoğlu, Istanbul2014Participant & Exhibition Designer — with Herkes İçin Mimarlık

Curated by Marco Scotini and Andris Brinkmanis, with architectural setting by Herkes İçin Mimarlık. Disobedience Archive brings a comprehensive map of social conflict and a network of contemporary activism to town, shortly after the Gezi Park protests reshaped the struggle for the right to the city.
