Caution! Slippery Ground

Venue
Istanbul Modern
Year
2015
Role
Curator & Exhibition Designer — KOTUstudio
Plates
6

VitrA Contemporary Architecture Series presents an exhibition on architectural culture, coordinated by Pelin Derviş with exhibition design by KOTUstudio (Erdem Tüzün, Yelta Köm). Rather than voicing a single main idea, the exhibition represents a fragmented structure through stations dispersed across the venue.
