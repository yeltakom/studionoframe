Tirailleurs: Trials and Tribulations

HKW, Berlin, 2026 — Exhibition Architecture

Exhibition architecture for Tirailleurs: Trials and Tribulations at Haus der Kulturen der Welt (HKW), Berlin, curated by Paz Guevara and Bonaventure Soh Bejeng Ndikung. The exhibition reframes the marginalized histories of the Tirailleurs through works by contemporary artists alongside archival and thematic sections. The spatial language, developed in close collaboration with the curatorial team, opens the exhibition as a common space for celebration and listening. Photos: Hanna Wiedemann / HKW.
