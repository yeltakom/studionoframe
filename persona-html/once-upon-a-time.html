Füsun Onur: Once Upon a Time…

Pavilion of Turkey, 59th Venice Biennale · 2022
Exhibition Designer

Design and setup of the Pavilion of Turkey at the 59th International Art Exhibition — La Biennale di Venezia, showcasing Füsun Onur's mixed media installation Once upon a time… The installation's dynamic arrangement of metal wire sculptures, everyday objects, and delicate materials created a poetic and spatial experience; the role involved coordinating the spatial layout and managing the exhibition setup.
