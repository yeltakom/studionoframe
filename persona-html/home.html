Studio No Frame is a Berlin-based creative studio working across art direction, exhibition design, art production, project management, curation, and consulting. We collaborate with artists, cultural institutions, NGOs, project spaces, and universities to realize interdisciplinary and socially engaged projects.

With a flexible structure and a diverse team, we approach each project with care, curiosity, and a strong emphasis on dialogue. Our practice values collective thinking, experimental methods, and process-oriented making.

We have worked with a wide range of partners including Haus der Kulturen der Welt, Istanbul Foundation for Culture and Arts (IKSV), Istanbul Modern, Ludwig Museum, MAXXI Rome, Pera Museum, SALT, La Biennale di Venezia – Venice Architecture Biennale, Istanbul Biennial, Istanbul Design Biennial, and Timișoara Biennial. We have also collaborated with artists such as Abbas Zahedi, Ahmet Öğüt, Amy Lien & Enzo Camacho, Ayşe Erkmen, Füsun Onur, Gülsüm Karamustafa, Leman Darıcıoğlu, Monira Al Qadiri, Raul Walch, and Tomás Saraceno.

We believe in open conversations and sustainable production processes.
