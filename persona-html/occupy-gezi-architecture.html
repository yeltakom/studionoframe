#OccupyGezi Architecture

SALT Beyoğlu, Istanbul · 2014
Editor & Coordinator — with Herkes İçin Mimarlık

An archive project documenting the spatial practices of the Gezi resistance. Exhibited at SALT Istanbul, V&A Museum London (Disobedient Objects), nGbK Berlin, MAXXI Rome, the 2nd Istanbul Design Biennial, Hidden Playground Cologne, Adhocracy Athens, and the Art Institute of Chicago.
