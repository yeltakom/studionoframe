Gülsün Karamustafa: Hollow and Broken—A State of the World

Türkiye Pavilion, Venice / 2024 · Production

Exhibition design and spatial layout contribution for the Türkiye Pavilion at the 60th International Art Exhibition — La Biennale di Venezia, featuring Gülsün Karamustafa’s installation. The spatial strategy, developed with the curatorial team, reflects the layered and narrative structure of the work.
