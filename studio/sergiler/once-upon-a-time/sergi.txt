Mekân: Pavilion of Turkey, 59th Venice Biennale
Yıl: 2022
Rol: Exhibition Designer
Hat: monographs
Ana sayfa: hayır
Özet: Design and setup of the Pavilion of Turkey at the 59th International Art Exhibition — La Biennale di Venezia, showcasing Füsun Onur's mixed media installation Once upon a time… The installation's dynamic arrangement of metal wire sculptures, everyday objects, and delicate materials created a poetic and spatial experience; the role involved coordinating the spatial layout and managing the exhibition setup.

--- Açıklama ---
Design and setup of the Pavilion of Turkey at the 59th International Art Exhibition — La Biennale di Venezia, showcasing Füsun Onur's mixed media installation <em>Once upon a time…</em> The installation's dynamic arrangement of metal wire sculptures, everyday objects, and delicate materials created a poetic and spatial experience; the role involved coordinating the spatial layout and managing the exhibition setup.

Gestaltung und Aufbau des Pavillons der Türkei auf der 59. Internationalen Kunstausstellung — La Biennale di Venezia, mit Füsun Onurs Mixed-Media-Installation <em>Once upon a time…</em> Die Anordnung aus Drahtskulpturen, Alltagsgegenständen und fragilen Materialien schuf eine poetische Raumerfahrung; die Aufgabe umfasste die räumliche Disposition und die Leitung des Aufbaus.
