--- Açıklama ---
Projects with Studio Tomás Saraceno and the Aerocene Foundation, including <em>Aerocene Albedo</em> at Art Basel Miami (2018), <em>ON AIR — Carte Blanche à Tomás Saraceno</em> at Palais de Tokyo, Paris (2018), <em>Solar Rhythms</em> at Tanya Bonakdar Gallery, New York (2018), and <em>On the Disappearance of Clouds</em> (2019). Aerocene is a global community building a new ecology of practice towards an ethical collaboration with the atmosphere — free from borders, free from fossil fuels.
