Mekân: Pera Museum, Istanbul
Yıl: 2025
Rol: Exhibition Design & Installation Management
Hat: collections
Ana sayfa: hayır
Özet: Exhibition architecture for Feelings in Common: Works from the British Council Collection, curated by Ulya Soley, organized around the show's three chapters — “Preserving Care,” “Familiar Faces,” and “Future Perfect.” The spatial design shaped the galleries as chambers of resonance, where works by 29 artists — among them Lucian Freud, David Hockney, Sarah Lucas, Wolfgang Tillmans, Sonia Boyce, and Gilbert &amp; George — could meet across generations and mediums.

Ausstellungsarchitektur für <em>Feelings in Common: Works from the British Council Collection</em>, kuratiert von Ulya Soley, entlang der drei Kapitel der Schau — „Preserving Care“, „Familiar Faces“ und „Future Perfect“. Die Gestaltung formt die Säle als Resonanzräume, in denen Arbeiten von 29 Künstler:innen — unter ihnen Lucian Freud, David Hockney, Sarah Lucas, Wolfgang Tillmans, Sonia Boyce und Gilbert &amp; George — über Generationen und Medien hinweg aufeinandertreffen.
