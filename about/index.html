Profile

Studio No Frame designs exhibition architecture for exhibitions of contemporary art, architecture and research. The studio is based in Berlin and works with curators, artists, institutions, project spaces and universities.

The work covers exhibition architecture, production, artist collaborations, spatial research and curation. Each project starts from the curatorial concept and the existing building: the route through the rooms, how works are seen and read, and what can be reused or taken apart afterwards.

Institutions and artists

Haus der Kulturen der Welt, Istanbul Foundation for Culture and Arts (İKSV), Istanbul Modern, Museum Ludwig, MAXXI Rome, Pera Museum, SALT, La Biennale di Venezia, the Istanbul Biennial, the Istanbul Design Biennial and the Timișoara Biennial. Artists we have worked with include Abbas Zahedi, Ahmet Öğüt, Amy Lien & Enzo Camacho, Ayşe Erkmen, Füsun Onur, Gülsün Karamustafa, Leman Darıcıoğlu, Monira Al Qadiri, Raul Walch and Tomás Saraceno.

Team

The studio is led by Yelta Köm (b. 1986, Istanbul), architect and artist. Yelta Köm studied architecture at Yıldız Technical University in Istanbul and at the Städelschule in Frankfurt am Main. Biography and artistic work: yeltakom.info.

The team changes with each exhibition: designers, researchers and production managers are brought together for the work at hand.
