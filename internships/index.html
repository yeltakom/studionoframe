Paid internships, structured around real projects.

We offer remote, well-structured internships for students in design, architecture, art, cultural studies and related fields. We especially welcome students who need to complete a mandatory or curricular internship as part of their studies.

Our practice merges design research and spatial strategies, working at the intersection of architecture, cultural production and artistic experimentation. Projects range from conceptual frameworks and critical spatial practices to the realisation of exhibitions and public art installations, developed closely with artists, designers and researchers.

We do not offer unpaid internships. We believe in fair compensation and a supportive, structured experience where your work is valued.

To apply, send your portfolio and CV to contact@noframe.studio, and tell us the dates you are available and whether the internship is required by your school.
